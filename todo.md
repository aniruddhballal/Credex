The supplier registration flow creates a user with role supplier but never creates or assigns a companyId, so supplier accounts remain unlinked to any company. The login and JWT generation assume companyId may exist but do not enforce its creation, and the frontend dashboard relies on user.companyId to determine company linkage. As a result, newly registered suppliers successfully authenticate but always appear as having “No company linked to your account” because no company document is created or associated during or after registration.
